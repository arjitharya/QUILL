If things feel like too much right now

Quill can listen, but it can't keep you safe in a crisis. If you're thinking about harming yourself or are in immediate danger, please reach out right now:

US & Canada: call or text 988
UK & ROI: 116 123 (Samaritans)
Elsewhere: search "crisis line" + your country, or contact local emergency services.

If there's a person you trust - family, a neighbor, a doctor - reaching out to them matters too, even if it feels hard.
